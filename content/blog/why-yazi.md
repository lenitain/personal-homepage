# Why Yazi?

Most file managers try to do too much. Yazi does one thing well:
it shows you your files and gets out of the way.

## Speed

Written in Rust. Async I/O everywhere. It feels instant.

## Design

The three-column layout (parent / current / preview) is genius.
You always know where you are and what's coming next.

## Extensibility

Lua plugins. Custom previewers. It grows with you.

> "A good tool is an extension of your hand."
